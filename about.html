OUR STORY

About IDOSTEM LABS

On a mission to make 3D robotics education accessible, affordable, and exciting for every student in India.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower every school and college in India with world-class 3D robotics labs and STEM education, creating a generation of innovators and problem solvers who can compete globally.

🔭

Our Vision

A future where every student — regardless of geography or background — has access to hands-on robotics and technology education that prepares them for the world of tomorrow.

JOURNEY

Our Timeline

2020

The Beginning

IDOSTEM LABS was founded with a vision to bring robotics to every classroom in India.

First 10 Schools

2023

2026

Going Global

Expanding to international markets while deepening our STEM lab presence across India.
